Flight

  • W / S — pitch down / up
  • Q / E — roll left / right
  • A / D — yaw (rudder)
  • SHIFT / CTRL — throttle up / brake
  • CTRL + S — high-G turn (tighter pitch, bleeds speed)

Combat

  • SPACE — cannon (now a finite magazine — watch the GUN count)
  • A GUNSIGHT PIPPER computes cannon lead on the nearest target ahead — fly your nose onto the pipper and it snaps to a green GUNS diamond when you have a kill shot. Toggle in Settings.
  • G — fire missile (homes only with a full lock)
  • X — drop flares (now reliably drag incoming missiles off you)
  • F — designate / cycle lock target (locking is manual by default — toggle Auto-Lock in Settings)
  • R — special ability
  • C cycle camera (incl. clear cockpit view) · V hold to look back · hold RMB track
  • Fly into glowing green SUPPLY CRATES to restock guns, missiles & flares.
  • H / ESC — open this manual (pauses)

Target Lock

By default locking is manual: press F to designate the nearest target ahead (press again to cycle). A ring then closes while you hold it in the forward reticle; when it snaps to a solid red LOCKED box, your next missile homes and destroys any non-boss instantly. Bosses must be worn down. Clouds and active stealth break a lock. Prefer the old behaviour? Turn on Auto-Lock in Settings.

Aircraft Stats

  • SPD — top speed
  • AGI — turn rate / maneuverability
  • ACC — acceleration & throttle response
  • ARM — hull integrity (hit points)
  • STL — stealth: how well clouds & active cloak hide you, and how readily incoming missiles lose your lock
  • FPW — firepower: cannon damage & missile loadout. Each airframe also has a unique passive strength & weakness — see its card.

HUD & Radar

  • Red brackets + health bar mark enemies; brightest = nearest threat.
  • Edge arrows with distance point to off-screen contacts.
  • Radar is heading-up: dots = bearing, ticks = above/below you, yellow ring = locked target.
  • Heed PULL UP, MISSILE (pop flares), HIGH-G, STEALTH.

Your Wingman

  • By default every sortie launches with a loyal AI escort (teal jet, teal radar marker) — you can switch this starting wingman off in Settings. It flies formation off your wing and breaks to dogfight whenever an enemy comes into range, hosing fighters with its cannon and loosing the occasional missile.
  • It draws enemy fire — hostiles will sometimes shoot at it instead of you. It can be shot down (and a boss SHOCKWAVE PULSE will swat it), after which it regroups and a fresh escort returns on station a few seconds later. The bottom-right chip shows ON STATION or a REGROUPING timer.
  • The WING COMMANDER and SQUADRON tech-tree nodes add a 2nd and 3rd permanent escort and up-armour the whole flight; the FLEET COMMANDER capstone makes your escorts hit much harder. The F-47's CCA SWARM deploys three vivid electric-blue drones in front of you for ~16s — they immediately acquire targets and attack. The F-47's passive makes all AI escorts and CCAs hit 25% harder.

Enemies & Abilities

  • Red fighters hold their distance and circle, then commit to strafing gun-runs — they now carry a real cannon magazine and a single missile, so expect to be shot at.
  • Crimson drones — cheap kamikaze swarms that ignore guns and simply ram you. Individually fragile, lethal in numbers — keep moving, hose them with the cannon, or break line-of-sight in a cloud.
  • Gold ACE — an elite with more ammo that shrugs off a single missile; wear it down.
  • Bombers pack a heavy cannon, two missiles & a few flares.
  • Orange turrets are ground SAM sites (missiles only).
  • Magenta BOSS (every 4th wave) now cycles telegraphed special attacks — radial MISSILE BARRAGE, deployed DRONE SWARMS, and a close-range SHOCKWAVE PULSE — then enrages at low health. Watch for the warning, then break away or pop countermeasures.
  • Enemy ammo still runs dry — a disarmed foe is just a target.

R&D Tech Tree

You earn research points (RP) — shown as R&D on the HUD — only from damage, kills and assists you personally deal. Kills your wingmen or CCA drones score earn you nothing. Clear a wave and an R&D screen opens: spend RP across five branches (Gunnery, Missiles, Airframe, Electronic Warfare, Command). Each branch is a track — a node only unlocks once the one above it is bought. Purchases persist for the whole run, so every sortie builds a different fighter. Buy as many as you can afford, or none at all — press DEPLOY (or Enter) to launch the next wave.
